# Service Bulletin SB-2026-016: TD-40 condenser fin cleaning interval

## Reason {#reason}

This bulletin follows high dew point alarms in summer. It changes the condenser fin cleaning interval on the TD-40 from 1,000 hours to 500 hours.

## Maintenance intervals (reissued) {#maintenance-intervals}

On the TD-40, clean the condenser fins every 500 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the TD-40, fit the drain valve service kit every 8,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the TD-40, replace the pre-filter element every 2,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the TD-40, leak-check the refrigerant circuit every 4,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

Shorten every TD-40 interval by half on sites with high dust or ambient air above 40 °C.

## Applies to {#applies-to}

This bulletin supersedes the Maintenance intervals section of the TD-40 Service Manual, revision C, for every TD-40 unit in the field.
