# Service Bulletin SB-2026-013: KR-55 motor mount bolts torque

## Reason {#reason}

This bulletin follows motor mounts cracking on units with high vibration. It changes the motor mount bolts torque on the KR-55 from 70 Nm to 80 Nm.

## Torque values (reissued) {#torque-values}

Airend coupling bolts on the KR-55: tighten to 135 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Motor mount bolts on the KR-55: tighten to 80 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Discharge flange bolts on the KR-55: tighten to 80 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Oil filter housing on the KR-55: tighten to 25 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Cooler fan hub on the KR-55: tighten to 30 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Use a calibrated torque wrench and clean, dry threads on the KR-55 unless a value above says lubricated.

## Applies to {#applies-to}

This bulletin supersedes the Torque values section of the KR-55 Service Manual, revision C, for every KR-55 unit in the field.
