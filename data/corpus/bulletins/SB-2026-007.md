# Service Bulletin SB-2026-007: KR-110 separator element interval

## Reason {#reason}

This bulletin follows separator elements clogging early in dusty sites. It changes the separator element interval on the KR-110 from 8,000 hours to 4,000 hours.

## Maintenance intervals (reissued) {#maintenance-intervals}

On the KR-110, change the oil every 4,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-110, replace the air filter element every 2,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-110, replace the oil filter element every 2,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-110, replace the separator element every 4,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-110, inspect the drive belt every 2,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

Shorten every KR-110 interval by half on sites with high dust or ambient air above 40 °C.

## Applies to {#applies-to}

This bulletin supersedes the Maintenance intervals section of the KR-110 Service Manual, revision C, for every KR-110 unit in the field.
