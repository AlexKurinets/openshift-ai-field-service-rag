# Service Bulletin SB-2026-019: TD-75 drain valve service kit part number

## Reason {#reason}

This bulletin follows a redesigned drain valve seal. It changes the drain valve service kit part number on the TD-75 from 4434-7779 to 4434-8996.

## Parts (reissued) {#parts}

Drain valve service kit for the TD-75: part 4434-8996, which replaces part 4434-7779.

Condenser fan motor for the TD-75: part 4434-3725.

Pre-filter element for the TD-75: part 4434-3059.

Dew point sensor for the TD-75: part 4434-3368.

Order TD-75 parts through the service portal with the unit serial number.

## Applies to {#applies-to}

This bulletin supersedes the Parts section of the TD-75 Service Manual, revision C, for every TD-75 unit in the field.
