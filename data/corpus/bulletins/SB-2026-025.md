# Service Bulletin SB-2026-025: TD-40 refrigerant service valve caps torque

## Reason {#reason}

This bulletin follows slow refrigerant leaks at the valve caps. It changes the refrigerant service valve caps torque on the TD-40 from 10 Nm to 14 Nm.

## Torque values (reissued) {#torque-values}

Condenser fan hub on the TD-40: tighten to 10 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Refrigerant service valve caps on the TD-40: tighten to 14 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Drain valve body on the TD-40: tighten to 40 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Heat exchanger mounting bolts on the TD-40: tighten to 27 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Use a calibrated torque wrench and clean, dry threads on the TD-40 unless a value above says lubricated.

## Applies to {#applies-to}

This bulletin supersedes the Torque values section of the TD-40 Service Manual, revision C, for every TD-40 unit in the field.
