# Service Bulletin SB-2026-010: KR-75 oil filter element part number

## Reason {#reason}

This bulletin follows a supplier change for the oil filter element. It changes the oil filter element part number on the KR-75 from 4434-2770 to 4434-4568.

## Parts (reissued) {#parts}

Oil filter element for the KR-75: part 4434-4568, which replaces part 4434-2770.

Air filter element for the KR-75: part 4434-2557.

Separator element for the KR-75: part 4434-1862.

Drive belt for the KR-75: part 4434-6002.

Discharge temperature sensor for the KR-75: part 4434-3581.

Order KR-75 parts through the service portal with the unit serial number.

## Applies to {#applies-to}

This bulletin supersedes the Parts section of the KR-75 Service Manual, revision C, for every KR-75 unit in the field.
