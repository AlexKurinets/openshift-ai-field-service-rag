# TD-75 Service Manual

## Specifications {#specifications}

The TD-75 rated flow is 21.0 m³/min.

The TD-75 pressure dew point is 5 °C.

The TD-75 refrigerant charge is 1.69 kg of R-513A.

The TD-75 maximum inlet temperature is 60 °C.

The TD-75 shipping weight is 380 kg.

All TD-75 figures are at 20 °C ambient and sea level unless a table says otherwise.

## Torque values {#torque-values}

Condenser fan hub on the TD-75: tighten to 18 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Refrigerant service valve caps on the TD-75: tighten to 20 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Drain valve body on the TD-75: tighten to 40 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Heat exchanger mounting bolts on the TD-75: tighten to 29 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Use a calibrated torque wrench and clean, dry threads on the TD-75 unless a value above says lubricated.

## Fault codes {#fault-codes}

F5627 on the TD-75, high pressure dew point: the dew point stayed above 10 °C for 15 minutes. Action: check the condenser for dirt and the refrigerant pressures.

F1840 on the TD-75, condenser fan failure: the fan motor drew no current when called. Action: check the fan capacitor and the fan motor windings.

F5507 on the TD-75, low refrigerant pressure: suction pressure fell below the cut-out setting. Action: leak-test the refrigerant circuit before recharging.

F6200 on the TD-75, high refrigerant pressure: discharge pressure passed the high-pressure switch setting. Action: clean the condenser and check that the fan runs.

F8787 on the TD-75, drain valve blocked: no condensate discharge was detected for 30 minutes. Action: clean the drain valve strainer and fit the service kit if it is worn.

F3143 on the TD-75, ambient temperature high: ambient air passed 45 °C at the condenser inlet. Action: improve ventilation around the dryer.

## Maintenance intervals {#maintenance-intervals}

On the TD-75, clean the condenser fins every 1,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the TD-75, fit the drain valve service kit every 8,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the TD-75, replace the pre-filter element every 2,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the TD-75, leak-check the refrigerant circuit every 8,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

Shorten every TD-75 interval by half on sites with high dust or ambient air above 40 °C.

## Parts {#parts}

Drain valve service kit for the TD-75: part 4434-7779.

Condenser fan motor for the TD-75: part 4434-3725.

Pre-filter element for the TD-75: part 4434-3059.

Dew point sensor for the TD-75: part 4434-3368.

Order TD-75 parts through the service portal with the unit serial number.

## Procedures {#procedures}

Before any work on the TD-75, isolate the supply and depressurize the air circuit. Refrigerant work is for certified technicians only.

To clean the condenser on the TD-75, remove the front panel, blow the fins clear from the inside out with dry compressed air below 6 bar and check the fan blades for damage.

To service the drain valve on the TD-75, close the isolation valve, release pressure with the test button, remove the strainer and fit the parts from the service kit.
