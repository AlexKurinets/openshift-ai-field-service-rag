# KR-110 Service Manual

## Specifications {#specifications}

The KR-110 rated power is 110 kW.

The KR-110 maximum working pressure is 10 bar.

The KR-110 free air delivery is 18.7 m³/min.

The KR-110 oil capacity is 58 L.

The KR-110 shipping weight is 1,870 kg.

All KR-110 figures are at 20 °C ambient and sea level unless a table says otherwise.

## Torque values {#torque-values}

Airend coupling bolts on the KR-110: tighten to 125 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Motor mount bolts on the KR-110: tighten to 100 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Discharge flange bolts on the KR-110: tighten to 65 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Oil filter housing on the KR-110: tighten to 30 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Cooler fan hub on the KR-110: tighten to 35 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Use a calibrated torque wrench and clean, dry threads on the KR-110 unless a value above says lubricated.

## Fault codes {#fault-codes}

F9788 on the KR-110, high discharge temperature: the airend discharge passed 110 °C. Action: check the oil level and clean the cooler fins before restarting.

F2816 on the KR-110, low oil pressure: oil pressure fell below 1.5 bar while loaded. Action: check the oil level and the oil filter element.

F9173 on the KR-110, motor overload: the main motor overload relay tripped. Action: measure the motor current on all three phases and check the supply voltage.

F8208 on the KR-110, phase loss: one supply phase dropped out. Action: check the incoming fuses and the phase sequence relay.

F5119 on the KR-110, separator differential pressure high: pressure drop across the separator passed 1 bar. Action: replace the separator element.

F3834 on the KR-110, inlet valve fails to open: the inlet valve stayed closed on a load command. Action: check the solenoid coil and the control air line.

F1479 on the KR-110, discharge temperature sensor open circuit: the controller reads no signal from the sensor. Action: check the sensor plug and replace the sensor if its resistance is out of range.

F6635 on the KR-110, pressure transducer out of range: the line pressure signal is outside 4 to 20 mA. Action: check the transducer wiring and replace the transducer.

F6580 on the KR-110, emergency stop pressed: the emergency stop circuit is open. Action: release the button, find out why it was pressed and reset at the controller.

## Maintenance intervals {#maintenance-intervals}

On the KR-110, change the oil every 4,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-110, replace the air filter element every 2,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-110, replace the oil filter element every 2,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-110, replace the separator element every 8,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-110, inspect the drive belt every 2,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

Shorten every KR-110 interval by half on sites with high dust or ambient air above 40 °C.

## Procedures {#procedures}

Before any work on the KR-110, isolate the main supply, lock it out and vent the separator tank until the gauge reads zero. Wait ten minutes for the oil to cool below 60 °C.

To replace the separator element on the KR-110, remove the tank cover bolts in sequence, lift out the old element with its gaskets, fit the new element with new gaskets and refit the cover. Run the unit loaded for 15 minutes and check for leaks.

To change the oil on the KR-110, drain it warm through the drain valve into a container, replace the oil filter element, refill to the middle of the sight glass and run the unit for five minutes before topping up.
