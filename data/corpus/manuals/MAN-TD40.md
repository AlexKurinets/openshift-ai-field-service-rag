# TD-40 Service Manual

## Specifications {#specifications}

The TD-40 rated flow is 11.2 m³/min.

The TD-40 pressure dew point is 5 °C.

The TD-40 refrigerant charge is 1.07 kg of R-513A.

The TD-40 maximum inlet temperature is 65 °C.

The TD-40 shipping weight is 240 kg.

All TD-40 figures are at 20 °C ambient and sea level unless a table says otherwise.

## Torque values {#torque-values}

Condenser fan hub on the TD-40: tighten to 10 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Refrigerant service valve caps on the TD-40: tighten to 10 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Drain valve body on the TD-40: tighten to 40 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Heat exchanger mounting bolts on the TD-40: tighten to 27 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Use a calibrated torque wrench and clean, dry threads on the TD-40 unless a value above says lubricated.

## Fault codes {#fault-codes}

F4457 on the TD-40, high pressure dew point: the dew point stayed above 10 °C for 15 minutes. Action: check the condenser for dirt and the refrigerant pressures.

F4034 on the TD-40, condenser fan failure: the fan motor drew no current when called. Action: check the fan capacitor and the fan motor windings.

F1884 on the TD-40, low refrigerant pressure: suction pressure fell below the cut-out setting. Action: leak-test the refrigerant circuit before recharging.

F1295 on the TD-40, high refrigerant pressure: discharge pressure passed the high-pressure switch setting. Action: clean the condenser and check that the fan runs.

F1532 on the TD-40, drain valve blocked: no condensate discharge was detected for 30 minutes. Action: clean the drain valve strainer and fit the service kit if it is worn.

F7324 on the TD-40, ambient temperature high: ambient air passed 45 °C at the condenser inlet. Action: improve ventilation around the dryer.

## Maintenance intervals {#maintenance-intervals}

On the TD-40, clean the condenser fins every 1,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the TD-40, fit the drain valve service kit every 8,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the TD-40, replace the pre-filter element every 2,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the TD-40, leak-check the refrigerant circuit every 4,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

Shorten every TD-40 interval by half on sites with high dust or ambient air above 40 °C.

## Parts {#parts}

Drain valve service kit for the TD-40: part 4434-6177.

Condenser fan motor for the TD-40: part 4434-9661.

Pre-filter element for the TD-40: part 4434-2408.

Dew point sensor for the TD-40: part 4434-9416.

Order TD-40 parts through the service portal with the unit serial number.

## Procedures {#procedures}

Before any work on the TD-40, isolate the supply and depressurize the air circuit. Refrigerant work is for certified technicians only.

To clean the condenser on the TD-40, remove the front panel, blow the fins clear from the inside out with dry compressed air below 6 bar and check the fan blades for damage.

To service the drain valve on the TD-40, close the isolation valve, release pressure with the test button, remove the strainer and fit the parts from the service kit.
