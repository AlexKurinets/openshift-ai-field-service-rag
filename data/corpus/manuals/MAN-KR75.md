# KR-75 Service Manual

## Specifications {#specifications}

The KR-75 rated power is 75 kW.

The KR-75 maximum working pressure is 10 bar.

The KR-75 free air delivery is 13.1 m³/min.

The KR-75 oil capacity is 42 L.

The KR-75 shipping weight is 1,340 kg.

All KR-75 figures are at 20 °C ambient and sea level unless a table says otherwise.

## Torque values {#torque-values}

Airend coupling bolts on the KR-75: tighten to 135 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Motor mount bolts on the KR-75: tighten to 105 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Discharge flange bolts on the KR-75: tighten to 70 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Oil filter housing on the KR-75: tighten to 45 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Cooler fan hub on the KR-75: tighten to 35 Nm in a cross pattern, then check again after the first 50 running hours.

Use a calibrated torque wrench and clean, dry threads on the KR-75 unless a value above says lubricated.

## Fault codes {#fault-codes}

F1257 on the KR-75, high discharge temperature: the airend discharge passed 110 °C. Action: check the oil level and clean the cooler fins before restarting.

F8103 on the KR-75, low oil pressure: oil pressure fell below 1.5 bar while loaded. Action: check the oil level and the oil filter element.

F4564 on the KR-75, motor overload: the main motor overload relay tripped. Action: measure the motor current on all three phases and check the supply voltage.

F6721 on the KR-75, phase loss: one supply phase dropped out. Action: check the incoming fuses and the phase sequence relay.

F4867 on the KR-75, separator differential pressure high: pressure drop across the separator passed 1 bar. Action: replace the separator element.

F2672 on the KR-75, inlet valve fails to open: the inlet valve stayed closed on a load command. Action: check the solenoid coil and the control air line.

F1826 on the KR-75, discharge temperature sensor open circuit: the controller reads no signal from the sensor. Action: check the sensor plug and replace the sensor if its resistance is out of range.

F9213 on the KR-75, pressure transducer out of range: the line pressure signal is outside 4 to 20 mA. Action: check the transducer wiring and replace the transducer.

F2375 on the KR-75, emergency stop pressed: the emergency stop circuit is open. Action: release the button, find out why it was pressed and reset at the controller.

## Maintenance intervals {#maintenance-intervals}

On the KR-75, change the oil every 2,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-75, replace the air filter element every 1,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-75, replace the oil filter element every 4,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-75, replace the separator element every 4,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

On the KR-75, inspect the drive belt every 2,000 hours of running time or once a year, whichever comes first.

Shorten every KR-75 interval by half on sites with high dust or ambient air above 40 °C.

## Procedures {#procedures}

Before any work on the KR-75, isolate the main supply, lock it out and vent the separator tank until the gauge reads zero. Wait ten minutes for the oil to cool below 60 °C.

To replace the separator element on the KR-75, remove the tank cover bolts in sequence, lift out the old element with its gaskets, fit the new element with new gaskets and refit the cover. Run the unit loaded for 15 minutes and check for leaks.

To change the oil on the KR-75, drain it warm through the drain valve into a container, replace the oil filter element, refill to the middle of the sight glass and run the unit for five minutes before topping up.
